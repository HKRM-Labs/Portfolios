# Fashion & Runway Model Editorial Portfolio Skill

## 1. Domain Guidelines for Modeling Portfolios
A high-caliber model portfolio differs fundamentally from tech or corporate websites. It serves two primary audiences:
1. **Casting Directors & Modeling Agencies**: Need instant access to authentic stats, un-retouched polaroids / digitals, comp card PDF, and verified measurements.
2. **Fashion Brands & Creative Directors**: Need visual impact, emotional range, lookbook versatility, runway walk / reel presence, and campaign track record.

## 2. Standard Digital Comp Card (Composite Sheet) Specifications
- **Core Measurements / Stats Block**:
  - Height: e.g., 5'8" (173 cm)
  - Bust / Chest: e.g., 34B
  - Waist: e.g., 26 in (66 cm)
  - Hips: e.g., 36 in (91 cm)
  - Shoes: e.g., 6 UK / 39 EU
  - Eyes: Dark Brown / Hazel
  - Hair: Dark Brown / Natural Waves
- **PDF Export / Download**:
  - One-click PDF Comp Card generation or download button directly in the Hero / Stats / CTA sections.
  - Standard 5.5" x 8.5" (or A5) high-res printable format layout containing:
    1. Primary Headshot
    2. Full-length fashion look
    3. Profile / 3/4 beauty shot
    4. Traditional / Editorial drape shot
    5. Contact & Agency Representation information

## 3. Lookbook & Campaign Categorization
- **Haute Couture & Heritage Weaves**: Handlooms, Kanjeevarams, Banarasis, and Royal Drapes.
- **High-Fashion & Western Contemporary**: Editorial streetwear, monochrome silhouettes, minimalist gowns.
- **Bridal & Festive Jewels**: Intricate lehengas, temple jewellery collaborations, soft glam beauty closeups.
- **Motion & 9:16 Reels**: Seamless video integration showcasing runway poise, fluid saree pleat motion, and backstage energy.
