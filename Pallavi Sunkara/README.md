## 🎨 Art Direction & Design System

- **Palette (Cinematic Velvet Plum & Warm Champagne):**
  - **Velvet Truffle Noir** (`#120E12` / `#181218`) — Warm, sensual photographic depth foundation replacing harsh cold black.
  - **Luminous Champagne Silk** (`#FAF3EB` / `#F4E5D4`) — Warm light silk text and high-contrast accents.
  - **Antique Rose Mauve** (`#E8A2B6` / `#D47A95`) — Creative couture blush accent for creative industry identity.
  - **Warm Apricot Nude** (`#EFA68F` / `#DD7F63`) — Warm textile and skin undertones.
- **Creative & Cinematic Typography:**
  - *Monumental Display & Headlines:* **Abril Fatface** — Voluptuous, curvaceous "bubble" high-fashion letterforms with intense dramatic thick-to-thin contrast.
  - *Avant-Garde Kinetic & UI:* **Syne** (Weights 600–800) — Modern French agency grotesque with sculptural geometric curves.
  - *Editorial Accents:* **Cormorant Garamond** & **Bodoni Moda** — Poetic italic ligatures.
- **Kinetic Motion & Animations:**
  - **Section 2 Scroll Runway Reel**: Right-to-left horizontal translation bound to vertical scroll progress via Framer Motion with complete frame containment.
  - **Section 3 Monumental 3-Row Kinetic Typography**: Full-screen alternating marquee strips gliding left-to-right and right-to-left.
  - **Autoplaying Videos**: High-definition video loops playing silently on loop (`autoPlay loop muted playsInline`) without play button clutter.
  - **Interactive Hover Tags**: Sleek floating tags revealing shoot credits, photographer tags, and engagement counts.
